## 🌟 Key Features

* **Realistic Pool Layout:** Official markings (2m offside, 5m/6m penalties & fouls, center, goals).
* **Player Management:** Drag & drop players (Blue & White teams), goalkeepers, and water polo ball.
* **Drawing Tools:** Freehand markers, straight passing lines, and movement arrows.
* **Phase & Animation System:** Step-by-step tactical sequences with smooth playback.
* **Preset Formations:** 6-on-5 extra player, zone defense, counter-attacks, and standard set plays.
* **Export & Save:** Save tactics as images (PNG) or files (JSON) to share with your team.
* **100% Offline & Ad-Free:** Operates anywhere poolside without internet access or interruptions.
